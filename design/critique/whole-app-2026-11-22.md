Run with `/impeccable critique everything` as two separate reviews: a design review of 43 screenshots, and measurements of the running app. Impeccable's own checker didn't run because its engine program isn't downloaded; the measurements were taken with Playwright instead.

The screenshots cover every tab plus goal detail, the add-expense sheet, sign-in, sign-up and forgot password. Variants: dark, light, Arabic, a 360 px phone, and a brand-new account.

Questions skipped: the owner asked for the fixes directly. The Home questions from `home-2026-11-22.md` were settled on their recommended answers: trust and first-run first, all issues, and Home as a short glance screen.

## Measurements (before)

- **Contrast.** Dark mode passes everywhere. Light mode fails in six places:
  - amber "Food: 92%…" 3.53:1;
  - the "Almost used up" pill 3.47:1;
  - the amber "500 EGP" 4.17:1;
  - category letters (red on red tint) 4.27:1;
  - muted text 4.48:1 on the page;
  - muted text 4.15:1 on `--card2`.
- **Tap targets under 24 px:**
  - "View all" (50×20);
  - "Show password", "Forgot password?" and "Create an account" (17–21 px tall).
- **Tap targets of 24–43 px:**
  - row delete buttons (34×34, 4 px from the rows);
  - chips (27 px);
  - the "Add" buttons (32 px).
- **Cut-off text at 360 px:**
  - Home's Income and Expenses amounts;
  - expense titles that lose space to two buttons per row.
- **The "+ Expense" button covers content mid-scroll:**
  - the "Add" button, 88–100%;
  - "View all", 100% at 360 px.
- **Headings and landmarks:**
  - every route jumps from h1 to h3;
  - the sign-in page has no `main` landmark;
  - in Arabic the tab title stays "Masrofy".
- **Small text:** bottom-tab labels are 11 px; tags are 11.5 px.
- **New account:** "Savings: Goal met, 0 EGP of 0 EGP" on Home and Budget.

## Priority issues and what was done

1. **[P1] Top card's maths didn't add up.** "83% used" and "Expenses 11,900" disagreed because saved and repaid money was counted but not shown.
   - **Fixed.** The card now shows the sum: Income − Spent − Saved − Repaid. Each line can be tapped to open where its number comes from, and Income has a "Change" hint.
   - The amount per day is in whole pounds.
   - The always-on trend icon is replaced by a true "9 days left".
2. **[P1] New account showed false success.**
   - **Fixed.** `rowMet` needs a plan above 0. A row with no plan and no activity says a neutral "No plan" in grey.
   - Home shows "Start with this month's income" with a **Set your income** button, instead of zero tiles.
3. **[P1] Add expense: the save button was below the fold.**
   - **Fixed.** In every sheet, a submit button at the end of its form now stays pinned to the bottom.
4. **[P1] Lists looked like a box inside a box.** The rows used the page colour inside a card-coloured frame.
   - **Fixed.** Rows now take the card colour.
5. **[P2] Home copied the Budget tab** (7 delete buttons, a full category list).
   - **Fixed.** Home now has a one-line "Budget usage" card ("3 of 7 categories need a look · 90% of plan", plus See all) and a Reports card.
   - The alert banner can be tapped to open Budget.
   - Income and Expenses moved into the top card.
6. **[P2] Delete buttons on every row** (Budget, Expenses, Loans, Savings).
   - **Changed.** They are now a quiet grey icon that turns red on touch, with a 36×44 tap area. Swipe-to-delete with Undo is unchanged.
   - *Not done:* removing them entirely. Keyboard and screen-reader users need a delete route first (e.g. "Delete" inside the edit sheet).
7. **[P2] Expenses looked different on Home and in Expenses.**
   - **Fixed.** Recent transactions use the category's own icon and colour, like the Expenses list.
8. **[P2] Loans colour meaning flipped.**
   - **Fixed.** Green now means a debt getting smaller (paid back or received back). A new loan is neutral. The +/− sign still shows cash in or out.
9. **[P2] Reports was hard to find.**
   - **Fixed.** Reports has its own card on Home.
10. **[P2] Raw figures.**
    - **Fixed.** Savings pace, average monthly spending and the daily allowance are in whole pounds (`wholeUnits` in `domain/money.ts`).
11. **[P2] Settings mixed risky and everyday actions.**
    - **Fixed.** "Delete my account" moved into Danger zone, next to "Erase all data".
12. **[P2] Light-mode contrast.**
    - **Fixed.** amber `#8a5a00` (lowest use 4.78:1), red `#9e2f2f` (5.57:1 on its tint), mute `#62637e` (4.95–5.35:1).
    - Spruce/indigo is unchanged: Space Indigo is the owner's chosen accent for light mode.

## Smaller items fixed

- Section headings are now h2 (no skipped level).
- The sign-in screens have a `main` landmark.
- The tab title follows the language (مصروفي).
- "View all" and inline text links have 44 px tap areas. "View all" uses the accent colour instead of the off-palette indigo.
- Bottom-tab labels are 12 px; tags are 12 px.
- Stat-tile amounts shrink a little on narrow phones instead of being cut off.
- The rings say what their % is of ("of your saving plan", "of the plan used").
- The donut legend is sorted biggest first and shows each share.
- The "+ Expense" button shrinks to a round icon once the page is scrolled.
- Chip rows fade at the edge to show they scroll.
- The tags placeholder is translated (#سفر #صحاب).
- The "goal no longer exists" screen has a Back to Savings button.

## Not done (for later)

- **Date fields show the phone's own format** (11/22/2026 in the test browser). This is the native picker. On a phone it follows the phone's language; a custom field is a bigger change.
- **Starter categories:** the defaults are still "Uni Transport & Food, Transportation, ETC, Hangouts".
- **Settings is still 14 cards.** Grouping it into Preferences, Data and Account is a separate job.
- **The tutorial is 11 steps** (suggested: 4–5).
- **The year table in Reports** still scrolls sideways on phones.
- **Removing row delete buttons** for good needs a delete inside each edit sheet first.
- **The "MK1" badge** means nothing to users. The owner decides its future.
- **The Android update check returns 404** on the test server (it has no APK). Expected locally.
- **A capture artefact:** the forgot-password text "Ask the person who runs this app…" appears only when email is turned off. With the default mail service on, it says the code was emailed.

## What's working

- The calm, consistent alert system: amber at 80%, red at 100%.
- Undo after delete, swipe, Log again, the maths keys and suggestions.
- The goal forecast ("At this pace you'll reach it in July 2027").
- Proper right-to-left layout and natural Egyptian Arabic.
- Reports' "Compared with last month".
